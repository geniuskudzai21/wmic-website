Our Story & Vision

Transforming financial complexity into strategic confidence since 2017

Understanding Dreams, Managing Wealth

WMIC Pvt Ltd isn't just about managing wealth—it's about understanding dreams. Since 2017, we've transformed financial anxiety into strategic confidence through integrated solutions that protect, grow, and sustain what matters most.

We serve ambitious individuals and visionary families, offering the sophistication of global finance with the personal touch of a trusted partner.

The Vision That Started It All

In 2017, Arjun Mehta, an entrepreneur who built his own fortune through disciplined investments, realized that true wealth wasn't just about accumulation—it was about intelligent protection and purposeful growth.

Arjun didn't want to create just another financial firm. He envisioned building a centre—both a literal and figurative space where insurance, property, investments, and financial planning would stop being isolated concerns and start working together like instruments in an orchestra to produce the best results.

Growth & Reach

From a modest office in Pretoria with three clients, WMIC has grown to serve over 2,500 families and 300 businesses, establishing ourselves as trusted partners in wealth creation and preservation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I prepare for my first consultation?

We recommend having information about your current financial situation, goals, and any specific questions you'd like to address. Don't worry if you're not sure what to bring—we'll guide you through the process.

How do you charge for your services?

We offer transparent fee structures tailored to your specific needs. This may include flat fees, percentage-based fees, or a combination. We'll discuss all costs upfront during our initial consultation.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes, we serve clients with international interests and can coordinate with professionals in other jurisdictions to ensure seamless cross-border wealth management.

How often will we meet to review my financial plan?

We typically meet quarterly for comprehensive reviews, with monthly check-ins for investment updates. However, we customize the frequency based on your needs and preferences.
